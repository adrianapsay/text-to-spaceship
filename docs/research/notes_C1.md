# C1 voxel/SDF — condensed notes
- Voxels/SDF = broad-phase placement engine, never validator. Conservative voxelisation (every touched cell occupied), cells shrunk by eps = 0.005 mm so Stage 1 contact legal. FFT correlation (Cui et al. 2023) CPU via SciPy pocketfft (GIL released, workers) -> per orientation full collision-free translation set + contact score. Physics terms closed-form per cell (affine/quadratic in t), no FFT.
- Resolutions: 2 mm interactive greedy decode; 1 mm background/refinement; 0.5 mm local windows + SDF tiles only (global 0.5 mm FFT ~0.5 GB/worker -> no).
- Per part once (cached by dataset SHA): canonical frame (tilted parts); conservative SAT triangle-box voxelisation at 0.5 mm (own vectorised; Akenine-Möller / Schwarz–Seidel) + scipy.ndimage.binary_fill_holes (26-separating shell); 1 mm & 2 mm via OR max-pool (exact, nested); 24 orientations via rot90/transpose (exact) + dedupe (sphere ~1, PCB/battery/rod/tank 3, box 6); narrow-band SDF tile 0.5 mm (libigl or Open3D).
- Per greedy step: rfftn of scene S; static obstacle grid (walls, rails, channel, PC/104 rods) -> static-feasible mask per (part, orientation) per session; collision C = irfftn(F(S) conj F(K)); contact P with 1-voxel outer shell kernel; feasible = C < 0.5 & static mask & fully inside; discard wrapped positions. Cost per cell weighted: Z-front increase − contact + COM distance (unplaced mass lumped at target) + trace inertia + products norm; argmin deterministic tie-break, keep top K. Stage 2 clearance: kernel cell occupied if sdf(centre) <= c + h sqrt3/2 (Lipschitz-safe); c = 2.2 parts, 1.2 statics. Stage 2 insertion mask = reverse cumulative-AND of (C == 0) along ±Z (stricter than paper's flood fill). Hand-off top layouts to exact layer: compact toward −Z/neighbours to 0.01 mm contact (Stage 1) or clearance + 0.01 mm (Stage 2) with FCL/manifold MinGap, full validation.
- Tight-fit exception: PCBs on PC/104 rods placed by rule (fixed XY, Z search) with exact checks: 90 mm board in 96 mm bay with 1.2 mm clearance leaves 1.8 mm/side < 2 mm grid overshoot (ESTIMATE).
- Cui et al. 2023 (full text read): collision = correlation of conservative indicator grids; proximity = correlation with Manhattan distance field; height penalty alpha z^3 (alpha 4 / 1e8); collision and proximity separate (ringing); O(n R N log N); brute 9.4 s vs FFT 0.003 s on 240x123x100 (RTX 3090 Ti cuFFT); 90° rotations best for axis-aligned sets 50.48% vs 45.39% at 30°, 104 s vs 2,100 s; interlock removal flood fill + ray-cast blocking graph; Thingi10K 6,596 meshes 35.77% interlock-free (40.28% without); vs Netfabb 50.45/34.71/6.89% vs 28.23/25.48/3.46%; CPU 313/342/425 s vs GPU 103/68/124 s (~3–5x); continuous refinement at 2 mm matches 1 mm density. Our grid 0.43 M cells at 2 mm (theirs 2.95 M at 2 mm).
- Soundness chain: solid ⊂ voxels -> voxel-disjoint => solid-disjoint (penetration <= 2 eps); nested grids -> coarse-feasible => fine-feasible => exact; free voxels certified empty -> voxel largest empty box = certified lower bound. Heightmap (Wang & Hauser max-plus dilation) cheap but misses undercut cavities -> fallback seed.
- Limitations: discretisation waste ~ A h/2; dataset surface area 2,173 cm2 (computed read-only) -> +217/+109/+54 cm3 at 2/1/0.5 mm vs 1,080 cm3; Z lower bound 108 -> ~130/119/113 mm; tight fits missed; discrete translations; free rotation re-voxelise; greedy myopic for COM/inertia; float32 FFT threshold 0.5 (float64 at 0.5 mm); clearance overshoot 1–2 voxels; never mass properties from voxels.
- Memory: 2 mm 50x50x171 = 0.43 M cells ~8 MB/worker; 1 mm 100x100x341 = 3.41 M ~65 MB; 0.5 mm 27.2 M ~0.5 GB. Spectrum cache 2 mm ~330 MB (190 unique orientations); 1 mm recompute.
- Timing ESTIMATE (3–5 GFLOP/s pocketfft UNVERIFIED): 2 mm 4–7 ms/transform, 10–15 ms per (part, orientation), ~190 evals/decode -> 2–3 s single thread, 0.25–0.5 s on 12 threads; 1 mm ~9x slower (2.5–4.5 s on 12); preprocessing 2–6 s once; first valid improved 0.5–1 s (2 mm decode + exact compaction); crop FFT along Z halves early N. - Tests: conservativeness (vertices, barycentric surface samples, 1e6 interior winding samples -> zero misses) at h in {2,1,0.5}; SAT vs SDF voxelisation cross-check; analytic box battery grid-aligned h=1 -> 140,800 cells, shifted 0.5 mm -> 81x81x23; rotation voxelize(R mesh) == rot90(voxelize(mesh)) all 24; hierarchy maxpool(V1) == V2; FFT vs brute force identical counts, float32 max err < 0.25 all-ones; soundness 10 seeds at 2/1 mm exact pairwise (Stage 1 penetration <= 0.01 mm; Stage 2 MinGap >= 2.2/1.2; zero false free); insertion fixtures capped vs open cavities; torus hole feasible rod positions vs analytic disc; physics map closed form vs independent 1e-9 at 100 cells; determinism; 2 mm decode <= 1 s on 12 threads.
