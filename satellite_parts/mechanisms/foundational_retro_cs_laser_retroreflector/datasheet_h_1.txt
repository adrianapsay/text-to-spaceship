MISSION BRIEFING
The Foundational Retro-CS laser retroreflector enables high-
precision tracking of satellites with Satellite Laser Ranging 
(SLR) up to 800km orbits for missions with a wide array of 
challenging CONOPS. The Retro-CS has been designed 
to enable easy integration into CubeSat missions where 
volume is at a premium.
The countersunk design allows the Retro-CS to sit almost 
flush to the skin of the CubeSat enabling compliance with all 
major dispensers.
