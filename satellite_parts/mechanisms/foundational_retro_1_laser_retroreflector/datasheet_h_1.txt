MISSION BRIEFING
The Foundational Retro-1 laser retroreflector enables high-
precision tracking of satellites with Satellite Laser Ranging 
(SLR) up to 800km orbits for missions with a wide array of 
challenging CONOPS. The Retro-1 has a simple lightweight 
design which requires no power or data from the host 
satellite. 
The Retro-1 is built and qualified to ensure performance and 
resilience over a diverse range of mission profiles and has 
TRL 9 flight heritage.
