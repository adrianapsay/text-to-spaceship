MISSION BRIEFING
The Foundational Retro-2 laser retroreflector enables high-
precision tracking of satellites with Satellite Laser Ranging 
(SLR) up to 1400km orbits for missions with a wide array of 
challenging CONOPS. The Retro-2 has a larger angle of 
acceptance than the Retro-1 and a larger effective optical 
cross section whilst maintaining a simple lightweight design.
Developed in collaboration with ESA to ECSS standards, 
the Retro-2 is built and qualified to ensure performance and 
resilience over a wide array of mission profiles and has TRL 9 
flight heritage.
