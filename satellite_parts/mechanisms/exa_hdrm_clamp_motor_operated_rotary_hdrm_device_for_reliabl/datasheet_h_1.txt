The EXA Clamp Hold down & Release Mechanism (HDRM-CLAMP) has been designed for the Small 
Satellite range (50 to 500 Kg) deployables like solar arrays, hatches or any other deployable 
mechanisms that need a safe, no-compromise, electrically powered HDRM. 
 
With a very small footprint (56x22x15 mm), surface mount, very low mass (32 g) and small power 
consumption (<1W), yet boasting a hold-down force of up to 48.9 Newtons and a retention force 
of over 245 Newtons, it has been tested to hold down solar arrays as big as 500x530x1500 mm 
during launch, its precious metals brush micro motor with 1000:1 gear box lubricated by industry-
standard Castrol®  Braycote®  601EF easily and safely withstand the conditions in LEO, MEO, GEO 
and Lunar surface, very simple to operate over a wide range of voltages (5 to 24V) and fast action 
(<1.5s) insures release of any deployables in space. 
 
Analog and digital versions are available, on the analog version, you just need to send a TTL signal 
to an SSR relay or a MOSFET allowing to pass 5 to 24V to the HDRM, depending on your 
deployment speed requirements. 
 
The digital version includes an integrated incremental quadrature encoder on the motor shaft. The 
two-channel Hall effect encoder senses the rotation of a 6-pole magnetic disc on a rear protrusion 
of the motor shaft, providing a resolution of 12 counts per revolution (CPR) of the motor shaft 
when counting both edges of both channels. To compute the gearbox output CPR, multiply 12 by 
the gearbox reduction factor. So, for the 50:1 gearbox, the encoder would provide approximately 
11837 CPR of the gearbox output shaft. Then you can know the position of the HDRM at any point 
and therefore report the successful opening and release of your deployable. All models are 
provided with Forward-Reverse switches for ground operation and testing.
