PowerBank-50  
Technical Specifications 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Description: 
 
PowerBank-50 is a modular CubeSat battery pack using high energy density lithium ion 
cells. Each pack contains 4 x 18650 cells, with a total energy capacity of over 50 Wh. The 
battery configuration can be either 2S2P or 4S1P. Multiple battery packs of the same 
configuration can be stacked together to increase the overall energy storage capacity of the 
satellite. The built-in battery management system optimally charges and discharges the 
batteries to maximise battery life and ensures constant power delivery to the system even 
when the batteries are charging.  An intelligent fuel gauge system monitors and logs the 
battery health and state of charge, balances the cells, and protects the cells from various 
fault conditions. Polyimide heaters are attached to each cell to ensure that the cell 
temperature does not drop below 5°C. The cells and electronics are packaged into a 
compact PEEK enclosure with an aluminium lid for thermal management. The pack provides 
rich telemetry and heater control through I2C protocol.
