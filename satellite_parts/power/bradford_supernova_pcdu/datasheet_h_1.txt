MAIN FEATURES
Bradford’s SUPERNOVA is a complete modular power 
management system, scaling power, outputs and 
features, making it the best solution for a wide variety of 
mission profiles and spacecraft designs.
The needs for high reliability, low mass and long lifetime 
are met by Supernova. It is built to be single-failure-
tolerant leading to increased resilience against single 
events and other type of failures. 
Supernova also performs best-in-class on the power to 
size and weight metric for a wide range of power 
configurations. All of this makes it the ideal candidate for 
deep space, high delta-v but also weight-, size- and 
reliability- sensitive LEO missions
As part of Bradford’s integrated system (BASIS), it can be 
included as a key element in a larger avionics unit that can 
provide power management, communications, RTUs and 
more – all while allowing your mission to skip the risk and 
effort of integrating disparate systems.
