Li-ion Space Battery

CM170 – 4s6p P20 14V 12Ah
Fully qualified for space applications, 
this battery has been utilised for a 
variety of nano/micro satellite missions, 
including a constellation for Internet of 
Things (IoT). 
The design incorporates a single D-type 
connector and does not require cell 
balancing electronics, making it easy to 
store, use and integrate into the spacecraft.
