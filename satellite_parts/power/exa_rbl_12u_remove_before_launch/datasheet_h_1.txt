The RBL 12U (Remove Before Launch) is a mechanically simple, minimalistic and very solid, CNC 
machined, sturdy and thick device with very few failure points. They are mechanically actuated by 
loaded compression springs and have contacts made of solid Beryllium Copper alloy 0.5mm thick 
for extreme redundancy. Contact between plates has an area of 115 mm2 and a hard casing made 
of PEEK. 
 
 
 
 
• Extremely simple and reliable design 
• Beryllium Copper 0.5mm thick plates actuated by compression springs 
• Very low part count 
• Sturdy, thick and over-engineered design that works every time, all the time 
• Very high electrical and thermal loads supported 
• Mechanically minimalistic, compression spring operated 
• Custom configurable choice of mechanical mating and screws  
• Designed for CubeSat (<50kg) applications, missions and requirements. 
• Manufactured according to NASA and ESA space standards and materials. 
• Repetitive functional and performance tests provided with documentation. 
 
 
 
• Weight (w/o RBL key): 34.5 grams 
• Conductive surface contact area: 115 mm^2 
• Dimensions (LxWxH): 60.00mm x 33.00mm x 16.00mm 
• Interface Flatness: +/- 0.01mm 
• Operating Temperature: -180C to +250C
