BAT-110  
The BST BAT-110 is a modular battery system for small satellites. It is based on Lithium-Ferrite 
(Li-Fe) cells. The BST’s Li-Fe battery technology offers 10x more cycle over Li-Io or Li-Po 
batteries and is significantly less hazardous. The battery block is protected for over charging 
(non-destructive), tolerant against deep discharge (non-destructive) and offers cell balancing. 
It has a design life of 5 years lifetime in LEO  and its direct predecessor is space proven.
