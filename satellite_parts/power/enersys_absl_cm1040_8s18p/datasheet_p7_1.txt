Li-ion Space Battery
ABSL™ 
CM1040 – 8s18p P20 28V 36Ah 
Fully qualified for space applications, 
this battery has been designed 
specifically for high power applications, 
such as Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) 
missions.  
The design incorporates two circular 
connectors for power and a D-type 
connector for telemetry and does not 
require cell balancing electronics, making 
it easy to store, use and integrate into the 
spacecraft.
