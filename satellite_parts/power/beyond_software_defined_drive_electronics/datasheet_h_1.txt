with its radiation-tolerant microcontroller and a fully software-defined

analog and digital sensor inputs and advanced driving profiles with micro-

The CODE+ is a compact, modular, high-reliable 
two-axes stepper motor controller capable of 
operating various types of mechanisms such as 
antennas, solar arrays or thrusters.
Each module provides 2 independent motor 
channels that can be individually configured as two- 
or three-phase drivers. Up to 3 sensors per motor 
are processed on-board or routed-through. Each 
sensor can be configured either as digital (switch) or 
analog input (thermistor, potentiometer). Additional 
2 feed-through lines are provided per motor, which 
can be used for functions not supported on-board 
such as heater control or encoder processing. Each 
motor interface is located on a separate connector. 
To increase the channel capacity, up to 4 modules 
may be stacked vertically.
Optional extension modules can be offered for e.g. 
multiplexing or MIL-STD-1553B interfacing. The 
CODE+ is controlled via a dual UART interface 
applying CCSDS packets. The unit is operated from 
a rad-tolerant microcontroller. The software is 
category B (ECSS-E-ST-40C) and supports in-orbit 
patching.
