The µHDRTX-K is a highly conﬁgurable multi-channel K-band transmitter 
that has two independent transmission paths. The transmitter implements 
DVB-S2 encoding and can support multi-channel transmission.
Each channel has a symbol rate of up to 150 Msps or 300 Msps in 
single-channel mode. Channel bonding is implemented which combines 
multiple channels to constitute larger  transmission bandwidths.
The µHDRTX-K is ideal for space missions that generate very large data 
sets such as multispectral imagers. This allows for payload data to be 
rapidly downloaded in a single pass and new data to be captured.
The adaptive modulation and coding of the transmitter enables optimized 
data throughput during a pass.
