The SpaceQuest TRX-V is a compact single board transceiver for satellite communications in the 
VHF Band. Similar to the TRX-U, the TRX-V is an ideal satellite TT&C Radio or Narrow Band 
Communication Payload for CubeSat and SmallSat missions.

130 MHz to 170 MHz

2.4, 4.8, 9.6, and 19.2 Kbps

1 to 16 Channels, Customer Programed in 300 Hz Steps

FSK, GFSK
