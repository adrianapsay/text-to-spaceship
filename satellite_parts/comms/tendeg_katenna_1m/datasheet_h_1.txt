About Tendeg 
Tendeg LLC provides antennas, precision deployable structures, and 
mechanical engineering design and analysis services for space 
missions. The company was founded in 2015 by a group of space 
industry veterans with an average of 22 years experience in designing 
and building space flight hardware. Tendeg has offices near Denver, 
Aspen, and Los Angeles.
