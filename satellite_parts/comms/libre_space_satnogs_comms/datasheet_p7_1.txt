The SatNOGS-COMMS is a dual-band software configurable radio transceiver specifically designed for
Telemetry and Telecommand (TMTC) in the S-band and UHF.
The radio module supports full in-flight reconfiguration of the carrier and
intermediate frequencies, bitrate, modulation options, and channel-filter bandwidth.
SatNOGS-COMMS is fully compatible with SatNOGS Network for all TMTC
functionality. The software and hardware are released as open source projects,
which can be tailored to user needs.
