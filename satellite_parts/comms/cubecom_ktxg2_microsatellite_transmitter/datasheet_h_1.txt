The KTXG2 is an extremely compact high-speed transmitter providing excellent 
data throughput performance. This next generation K-band radio’s design was 
based on making small but signiﬁcant improvements on our original XTX. Thus 
building on the ﬁrst generation’s legacy in performance and leveraging its 
signiﬁcant flight heritage, to inform design decisions. The addition of 32GB of 
onboard memory and dynamic re-transmit capability allows you to minimize 
your link margin excess and maximize your data throughput.
This exceptional nanosatellite transmitter has now been redesigned for the 
microsatellite formfactor. The µKTXG2 implements the DVB-S2 standard up to 
MODCOD 28 which supports QPSK, 8-PSK, 16-APSK and 32-APSK modulation 
at a symbol rate of 50 Msps. The maximum encoded data rate is 250 Mbps, 
making it the perfect transmitter for data intensive missions, constellations and 
any space mission that may require a high data rate downlink.
