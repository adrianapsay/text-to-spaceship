Frequency Converter for Ka-band payloads
Ka-band Frequency Converter for 30 GHz to 20 
GHz Conversion. The use of external LO and EPC 
allows the use of frequency converter with small 
size and low mass. The equipment is built using the 
latest MMIC technologies for excellent electrical 
performance while exhibiting low DC power 
consumption and small dimension.
