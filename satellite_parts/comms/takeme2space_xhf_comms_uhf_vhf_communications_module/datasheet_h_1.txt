Technical Specifications 
 
 
 
 
Description: 
The xHF Comms board is a communication module designed to facilitate robust and 
reliable communication between a satellite and a ground station. It operates in the Ultra 
High Frequency (UHF) band, with the specific frequency determined by the configuration. 
The UHF channel operates at 300-500 MHz. This capability allows for flexibility in 
communication, depending on the mission requirements, environmental conditions, and 
regulatory constraints.

The default configuration supports a data rate of 9.6 kbps using GMSK modulation over a 
14.4 kHz bandwidth in the 401–402 MHz frequency range.  
Communication Protocol and Mode 
The communication mode is half-duplex, meaning that the board can either transmit or 
receive data at any given time, but not both simultaneously. This mode is particularly 
suitable for satellite communication, where the ground station sends a telecommand (a 
command signal) to the satellite, and the satellite responds with telemetry (data about 
the satellite's status, health, and other mission-critical information). The half-duplex 
operation ensures that the communication is efficient and minimizes the risk of signal 
interference. 
Power Supply 
The xHF Comms board is powered by an isolated power supply, which is critical for 
ensuring the reliability and safety of the communication system. The isolation prevents 
electrical faults or noise from propagating through the system, which could otherwise 
disrupt communication or damage sensitive components. The input voltage to the power 
supply is 5V, which is a standard voltage level for many electronic systems, making it 
compatible with a wide range of satellite power systems.
