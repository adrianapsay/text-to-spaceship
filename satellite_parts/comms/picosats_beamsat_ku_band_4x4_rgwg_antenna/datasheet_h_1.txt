OVERVIEW
This cutting-edge Ku-band 4X4 antenna utilizes innovative Ridge Gap Waveguide 
technology, featuring a fully aluminium design manufactured through 3D printing. With 
high-gain performance, peak gain of  19 dBi, compact dimensions, less than 1 U surface, 
and a weight of  110 g. Its robust metal construction ensures durability and reliability, 
making it an ideal solution for missions requiring efﬁcient, high-performance 
communication in a lightweight and compact form, both for LEO and GEO applications.
