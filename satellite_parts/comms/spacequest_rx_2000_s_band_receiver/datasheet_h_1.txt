The  RX-­2000  is  an  efficient  space  qualified  FM  S-­Band  receiver  for  microsats  and  small  satellites.  
The  receiver  generates  a  clock  and  data  output  allowing  customers  the  flexibility  and  control  to  
implement  customized  or  reconfigurable  communication  protocols  on  their  flight  computer  or  on  an  
auxiliary  communications  processor.
