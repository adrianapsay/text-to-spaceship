PULSAR-XANT-PLUS 
 
HIGH-GAIN  
X-BAND ANTENNA 
 
 
The PULSAR-XANT-PLUS is a high-gain, lightweight, 
low-profile X-Band patch antenna designed for, but not 
limited to, CubeSat missions. It is compatible with the 
CubeSat standard in terms of height limitations for 
mounts on the exterior chassis. If your mission has a 
demanding link budget requiring high data-rates then 
the XANT-PLUS is the one for you, featuring a low 
profile, high-gain four element (2x2) array. 
  
KEY HIGHLIGHTS: 
• 
High data-rate for space missions needing to 
downlink high data volumes 
• 
High gain for space missions where greater link 
budget margins are needed, or simply to close 
the link 
• 
Sub 2 dB axial ratio over the entire X-Band of 
operation, with both LHCP and RHCP versions 
available 
• 
Extremely light-weight minimalistic design 
• 
Performance insensitive to the surrounding 
ground plane allowing seamless integration
