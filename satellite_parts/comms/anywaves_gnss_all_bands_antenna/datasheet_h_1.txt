GNSS All-Bands
Antenna
Anywaves GNSS all-bands antenna is perfectly suited 
for LEO platforms. Operating in the frequency band 
from 1.16 GHz to 1.61 GHz, our antenna covers all 
GNSS constellations simultaneously with its very 
stable phase center and circular polarization. This 
offers excellent signal stability, ensuring no signal 
blackouts occur even if one GNSS constellation 
breaks down.
This antenna has a TRL 8 and has successfully passed 
qualification tests. Developed under the European 
Space Agency Navisp contract, this antenna is reliable 
and efficient, giving you the accuracy and precision 
you need to ensure your satellite is always in the 
right position.
