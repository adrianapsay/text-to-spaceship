MAJOR INNOVATIONS
Sharanga is a next-generation micro-propulsion system
powered by a non-carcinogenic, green propellant - HTP.
With no pressurant gas needed, Sharanga is self-
pressurizing, operates at low system pressures, and offers
precise and throttleable thrust. Its low power demand
and lightweight design make it ideal for CubeSats and
small satellites. Sharanga provides simple, reliable and
cost effective solution for attitude control, orbit change,
station keeping, formation flying and deorbiting of
CubeSats.
