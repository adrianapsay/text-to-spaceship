 
 
 
 
 
The CosmoOrbit XE is a high-performance GNSS Receiver specifically designed 
for space applications. It offers a range of advanced features to ensure reliable global 
positioning and tracking in extreme environments with high precision requirements. 
 
 
 
Key Features: 
⚫ 
230 acquisition/tracking channels 
⚫ 
Support for global GPS, Galileo, GLONASS, QZSS, SBAS satellite systems. 
⚫ 
16 million time-frequency hypotheses testing per second 
⚫ 
Built-in active high gain antenna 
⚫ 
1PPS Output; 3.3V TTL, 100ms pulse width when 3D position fixed. 
⚫ 
4 GPS Tx indeports  
⚫ 
Operating temperature range: -40°C to +85°C 
⚫ 
Protocol: NMEA-0183 
⚫ 
Baud Rate: 115200bps 
⚫ 
Data Format: 8 bits, No Parity, 1 Stop Bit 
⚫ 
Default Datum: WGS-84  
⚫ 
User-definable Datum 
 
Electrical Specifications: 
⚫ 
Input Voltage: 5V 
⚫ 
Power Consumption:120mA 
 
Mechanical Specifications: 
⚫ 
Dimensions: 60x45x15mm 
⚫ 
Weight: 100g 
 
Environmental Specifications: 
⚫ 
Operating Temperature: -
40°C to +85°C 
⚫

to +125°C

The CosmoOrbit XE Series is built to withstand the rigors of space
