Description:​
​
The TakeMe2Space Reaction Wheels serve as a vital part of the satellite’s Attitude 
Determination and Control System (ADCS), enabling dependable and high-precision 
orientation management essential for mission success. 
 
Engineered for robust in-orbit performance, the RW-Medium model delivers a nominal 
momentum of 30 mNms and maximum torque of 75 mNm, supporting fine control 
accuracy of ±1 RPM at speeds above 100 RPM.
