Mag-13® 
Three-Axis
Magnetic Field Sensors
The Mag-13 range of sensors provide high precision 
measurements of static and alternating magnetic fields. 
Different combinations of noise level, measuring range, 
and a range of enclosures, make the sensors suitable 
for use in many applications. These include defence, 
physics, geophysics, bioelectromagnetics and mineral 
exploration.
