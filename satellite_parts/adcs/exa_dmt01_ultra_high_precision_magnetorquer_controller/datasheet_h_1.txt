DMT01 
ULTRA-HIGH PRECISION  
MAGNETORQUER CONTROLLER

The EXA DMT01 (Dynamic yield MagneTorquer controller) is a new kind of magnetorquer controller that enhances 
the classical PWM approach to manage magnetic-based ADCS actuators by allowing the user to dynamically change 
(up or down) the voltage and current being fed to the actuator via digital control (I2C). It can be used with or without 
PWM to control the integrated MT01 magnetorquer. 
 
In the DMT01/SP (Standard Precision) version the user can select dynamically the voltage levels as 1V, 2V, 3V, 4V 
and 5V, this allows a finer point control over the yield of the MT01 integrated into the controller allowing precisions 
up to 0.5 degrees. 
 
In the DMT01/HP (High Precision) version the same as above is true but the user can also feed a PWM signal 
concurrently from 1 Hz to 1100 Hz and from 1% to 100% duty cycle, allowing the MT01 to achieve a precision pointing 
accuracy of up to 0.01 (10E-2) of a degree and theoretically could achieve up to 0.001 (10E-3) of a degree with 
the right sensors. 
 
 
 
The controller has a very low consumption, high visibility green LED for each voltage channel that turns on/off 
when desired voltage is selected/deselected (i.e 2V, 3V, etc) so the user has a visual confirmation of the voltage 
selection, also, a sample C++ code is provided in order to test functionality.

 Duty cycle: 1% to 100% 
 Frequency supported: 1 to 1100 Hz (higher frequencies are available) 
 Digital voltage selection: 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5V (higher ranges are available) 
 Protocol: I2C  
 Selectable I2C addresses: 8 
 High-Speed I2C Interface:  100kHz, 400kHz, 1.7MHz 
 Onboard analog temperature sensor: LM50C, -55C to +125C 
 Digital Input power supply: 5V@100mA 
 Digital interface: Molex 53261-05 
 MT01 power supply: 5V@1.2A 
 MT01 interface: Molex 53261-03 
 
 
 
 
 
 Programming language: C++ 
 Compatibility: Arduino UNO 
 Parameters: Duty cycle, Frequency and Voltage channel on/off selection 
 Functions: Safety disable of concurrent channels, initialization, test. 
 Operability: Ground tests only, not for spaceflight 
 
 
 
 
 
 Working Voltage: From 1.25V to 5V 
 Working Current: From 70mAh to 1100 mAh 
 Nominal Magnetic moment: >0.558 Am2 @ 5V 
 Saturation Magnetic moment: >0.790 Am2 @9V 
 Linearity: +/- 4% across operating design range 
 Residual moment: <0.0005 Am2 
 Torque: 5.36 µNm @ 7.2-3 mTesla (1U mass) 
 Angular acceleration: 3.2-3 Rad/sec-2 (1U mass) 
 B-center = 18.9 Gauss @3v3
