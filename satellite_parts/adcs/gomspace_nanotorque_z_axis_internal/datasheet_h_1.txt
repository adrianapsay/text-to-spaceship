2 Overview 
A magnetorquer is used with a satellites ADCS. The GomSpace NanoTorque Z-axis Internal is 
made for nano-satellites. It has flown on several satellites and performed perfectly. The size is fitted 
for standard PC104 size. 
 
The top of the Z-axis Internal is defined as shown on the front-page picture. 
 
Below is a picture of the Z-axis Internal integrated into the top of the GOMX-3 satellite.
