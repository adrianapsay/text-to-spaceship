Space Wheel 30​
Reaction Wheel Assembly 
Technical Specifications 
 
​
Description:​
The Space Wheel 30 mNms Reaction Wheel Pyramidal Assembly is a complete, 
integrated attitude control actuator suite designed for cubesat and microsat platforms. 
The assembly comprises four brushless DC reaction wheels, each delivering 30 mNms of 
momentum storage, mounted in a pyramidal configuration that provides full three-axis 
control authority. The geometry allows torque and momentum to be distributed across all 
four wheels, improving agility.
