MTR are regularly used in Communication Satellites and Remote Sensing Satellites. They 
are predominantly used for momentum dumping i.e. de-saturation of momentum wheels 
and reaction wheels.Angular momentum and notation control of spinning momentum- 
biased and dual-spin spacecraft.
