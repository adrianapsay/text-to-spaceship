Magnetorquer
Rod-type electromagnetic actuator for attitude control and
momentum management
The Azista Magnetorquer is a rod-type electromagnetic actuator for propellant-free spacecraft attitude control and reaction-wheel 
desaturation, built on a design lineage ﬂown across GEOSAT, IRNSS navigation, and Earth observation platforms. A Permendur-49 
cobalt-iron core delivers high saturation ﬂux density and low coercivity integrated with dual independent redundant coils wound with 
over 1 lakh turns of polyimide-insulated copper wire. Each coil independently delivers the full rated dipole moment, while maintaining 
a residual magnetic moment below 1 A·m² to prevent parasitic disturbances when unpowered.
