The MAI Sun Sensor can be used for a variety of 
spacecraft missions requiring sun detection. The output 
of the MAI Sun Sensor is an analog voltage range which 
can be fed into a microcontroller ADC. It can also be fed 
to some signal conditioning circuitry to obtain an 
amplified voltage output of up to 3.3 or 5V. 
MAI Sun Sensors are sold in packs of 6 and are available 
in two sizes: SmallSat or CubeSat.
