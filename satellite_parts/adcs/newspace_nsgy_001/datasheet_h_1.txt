The NewSpace stellar gyroscope uses a COTS 
sensor and optics resulting in a very low-cost 
attitude determination system that maintains 
accuracy during the eclipse phase. It can 
achieve this by using algorithms that tolerate 
noise and does not require a star database. 
It is thus far more robust against radiation 
damage than a standard star mapper solution 
would be if based on the same components.
The NewSpace stellar gyroscope can be used 
to propagate a spacecraft’s attitude from a 
known initial condition, without drift, while 
suﬃcient stars are common across frames. 
The image-based rotation estimates can 
complement a set of MEMS rate gyroscopes 
to maintain a high accuracy attitude estimate 
at low angular rates (where MEMS gyroscope 
drift is most severe).
