VANTAGESUN Medium
USA-sourced and manufactured, the highly reliable 
VantageSun sun sensors have decades of 
spaceflight heritage. This low-cost, two-axis, 
medium-resolution sun sensor comprises a small 
housing and four quadrant photodiode assembly. 
The housing assembly also serves as an aperture, 
reducing mechanical complexity and precisely 
masking solar light.
• 2-axis accuracy better than 1° over full FoV
• Passive device draws no power
• Low mass less than 40g for all configurations
• Long and short baffle configurations
• Calibration data provided with each sensor
• Attitude determination during:
– Launch vehicle separation 
– Initial attitude acquisition 
– Emergencies and off-nominal modes
•
Sun vector determination
