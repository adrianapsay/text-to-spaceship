Spectral Sensor 
Technical Specifications 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Description: 
Spectral Sensor is a  compact, low-power spectral sensor designed for CubeSat 
platforms where mass and power budgets are tight. It measures reflected solar radiation 
to derive surface albedo across its spectral response band. The same measurement 
supports use as an Earth sensor, providing coarse nadir determination for attitude and 
pointing checks. A single I²C interface handles configuration, mode control, and data 
readout, minimising harness count and integration effort. Its small form factor and low 
quiescent draw allow continuous operation without impacting the platform power budget.
