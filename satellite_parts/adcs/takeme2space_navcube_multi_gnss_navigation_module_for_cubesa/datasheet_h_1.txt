Navcube (Dual Transceiver Model) 
Technical Specifications  
 
 
 
 
Description: 
NavCube is a compact, high-performance multi-GNSS navigation module developed by 
TM2Space for CubeSats and small satellite platforms requiring reliable position, velocity, 
and timing information. Supporting GPS L1, GLONASS L1, and Galileo E1 constellations, 
NavCube provides precise navigation data and high-accuracy Pulse-Per-Second (PPS) 
timing for orbit determination, mission synchronization, and spacecraft operations. 
Designed in a PC104-compliant form factor with dual isolated Full-Duplex RS-485 
interfaces, the module enables seamless integration with satellite avionics while ensuring 
robust communication and electrical isolation. Featuring low power consumption, a 
rugged hard-anodized aluminium enclosure, and qualification through thermal vacuum, 
vibration, and shock testing, NavCube delivers reliable performance in demanding space 
environments.
