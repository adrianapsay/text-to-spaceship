This 0.6U Attitude Determination and Control System 
features two Star Trackers, three reaction wheels, three 
electromagnets, a 3-axis magnetometer, and an ADACS 
computer for a stand-alone, Plug-and-Play attitude control 
system for small satellites. The configuration is compatible 
with the CubeSat standard. The Star Trackers are oriented 
either 180 or 90 degrees apart, and angled upward at 24 
degrees. Other mounting angles are available.
