BST GPS-110
The GPS-110 is a reliable computation unit with two GPS receivers and RS422 interface for
measuring the position and velocity in orbit. The fully redundant GPS receivers are able to work 
independently with two active GPS antennas and provide one position and velocity update per 
second.
The device operates on a single 12 V supply, drawing a typical of 250 mA including Skyfox GPS 
antennas, when both receivers are switched on. (antennas not part of GPS-110)
