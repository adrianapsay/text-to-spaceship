IMUCube 
Technical Specifications 
 
 
 
Description: 
The IMUCube is a high reliability MEMS inertial measurement module. It integrates multiple 
high-performance Inertial Measurement Units (IMUs) to provide redundant and accurate 
measurements of angular rate, acceleration, and orientation. The board enhances system 
robustness through sensor redundancy and cross-validation, enabling improved attitude 
estimation and fault tolerance.
