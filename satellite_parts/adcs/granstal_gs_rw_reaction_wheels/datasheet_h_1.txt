GranStalTM Reaction Wheels Solutions
GS-RW
Reaction
Wheels
The GS-RW series reaction wheels are low mass, low power reaction control wheels, which allow CubeSats and other pico- or
nano-satellites to control their attitude.
These reaction wheels are specifically designed for 1 to 3U CubeSat platforms, and they are also used in the iADCS-series of
attitude determination and control systems as well as the iACS series of attitude control systems.
