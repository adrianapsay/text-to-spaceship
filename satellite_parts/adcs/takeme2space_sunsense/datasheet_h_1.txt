SunSense – Coarse Sun Sensor​
 
 
​
Description: 
SunSense, our advanced sun sensor, gives you a wide sensing range and a desirable operational 
temperature, delivering precision and reliability. Its compact design makes it an ideal fit for any 
CubeSat configuration. Featuring a low shut-down current consumption, SunSense ensures efficient 
energy management. SunSense’s sensor includes on-board temperature compensation. It also 
includes a low-noise amplifier and a 16-bit ADC. It comes with a hard anodised AL 6061 enclosure, 
making it mountable externally on all solar panels.​
