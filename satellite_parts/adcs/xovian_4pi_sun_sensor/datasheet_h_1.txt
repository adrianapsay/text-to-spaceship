
4 π Sun Sensor 
for Low earth Satellites
4 π Sun Sensor is used in Spacecrafts to find the orientation with respect to Sun. Sun Sensor 
consists of two optical heads each head has solar cells mounted on three orthogonal sides. 
Two such heads mounted on two diametrically opposite side of a Satellite gives three di-
rection cosines.
4 π SUN SENSOR
There are two solar cells on each side for redundancy. A nine pin connector on each head 
brings out the solar cell out. Specification
* Each cell output is given as
    I = Imax COS θ where Imax + 8 ma +/- 10%
* Physical size : 46 x 30 x 32 mm
* Mass : 50 g
* I/F : 9 pin Micro D connector
