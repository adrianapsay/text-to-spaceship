RTK accuracy & High dynamics receiver

advanced algorithms for robust and accurate positioning.

+ 136 channels for tracking signals from GPS,
BeiDou, support single BD GNSS positioning

+ Centimetre-level (RTK) and sub decimetre-
level position accuracy
+ Support dynamic base station, used for two-
dimensional orientation, three-dimensional
attitude measurement

Accuracy scaleable to a centimetre
Gran Stal’s knowledge and experience in the GNSS
industry ensures that the GSD400 offers you the highest
possible accuracy, scaleable to a centimeter.

Beidou dual-band, two systems for joint positioning,
with ultra-long distance RTK solution engine, adapt to
harsher, long distance environment.
