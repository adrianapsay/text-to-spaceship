Technical Specifications 
 
 
 
Description: 
StarSense Lite is a compact, high-accuracy star tracker by TakeMe2Space for the 
CubeSat and small satellite class. Built ground-up in India, we design and fabricate the 
radiation-shielded lens assembly, electro-optical baffle, four-board read-out electronics, 
and precision opto-mechanical enclosure in-house — only the detector and select lenses 
are sourced externally. This depth of supply chain control eliminates the long lead times 
typical of traditional star trackers. 
 
At its core is a hybrid attitude-determination pipeline that pairs classical, flight-proven 
algorithms with proprietary AI models running on a dedicated FPGA + AI-accelerator 
board. Deep-learning based star detection and sub-pixel centroiding increase usable 
stars per frame and sharpen centroid accuracy, while remaining fully deterministic at
