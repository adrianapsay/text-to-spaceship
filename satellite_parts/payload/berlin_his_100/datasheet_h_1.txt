HYPER SPECTRAL CAMERA (OBSOLETE) 
In the frame of the Kent Ridge 1 mission Berlin Space Technologies has developed a dual 
band VNIR & SWIR hyper spectral payload. The instrument is built upon a proprietary 
technology from Singapore. A special filter element allows the camera to act as a polarizing 
Fourier transform hyper spectral camera (PolarFour™). The payload incorporates a high 
performance data processing unit and data storage (DPU) for each of the two bands. For 
utilisation on a high altitude platform a light weighting modification is proposed.
