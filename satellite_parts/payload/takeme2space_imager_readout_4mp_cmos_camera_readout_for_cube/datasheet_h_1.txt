CMV4K Readout 
Technical Specifications 
 
 
 
 
 
Description: 
The CMV4K Readout is a compact snapshot imaging system built around a 4-megapixel 
global-shutter CMOS image sensor, delivering RAW imagery at 8/10/12-bit depth or 
JPEG-compressed output. Image data is transferred over Gigabit Ethernet, with RS485 and 
CAN interfaces provided for telemetry and telecommand.
