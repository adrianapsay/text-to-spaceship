On board computers are designed for space use and are flight proven with 
extensive heritage and excellent reliability. The Next Generation On Board 
Computer (OBC NG) is a fully redundant high reliable on board computer based 
on the radiation hardened CREOLE ASIC developed by Beyond Gravity. The 
CREOLE ASIC is a complete spacecraft on board computer on a chip including 
a fault tolerant SPARC® V8 processor, communication functions, 
reconfiguration function and mass memory controller.
