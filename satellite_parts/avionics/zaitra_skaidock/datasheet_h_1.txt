DATA PROCESSING UNIT
SIMERA SENSE XSCAPE COMPATIBILITY
SKAIDOCK is fully compatible with Simera Sense xScape imagers. 
Its dedicated camera connector provides I2C, SPI, PPS, LVDS, 
and 5V power interfaces, with a pinout that matches the xScape 
specification. The standard Simera Sense harness can be used 
directly for integration. When combined with the Data Readout 
IP Core, the setup offers true plug-and-play functionality: you can 
command the camera and retrieve TIFF imagery with zero 
additional development effort.
MECHANICAL DESIGN 
The SKAIDOCK DPU is enclosed in an anodized aluminum housing, 
machined from 6061-grade alloy for optimal strength-to-
weight performance. It includes four mounting holes compliant 
with the PC104 standard, allowing secure integration into 
the spacecraft structure.

FEATURES
SKAIDOCK delivers high-performance data processing 
and handling for small satellite missions. Designed for CubeSats, 
it offers seamless integration and plug-and-play compatibility 
with Simera Sense xScape imagers, making it the ideal choice 
for modern Earth Observation missions.
At its core, SKAIDOCK features the Xiphos Q8 module powered 
by a Zynq UltraScale+ MPSoC. With flight heritage from hundreds 
of missions, the Q8 ensures trusted reliability in space 
environments. SKAIDOCK includes redundant system and data 
storage for increased mission resilience. Its versatile interface 
suite, ranging from RS422 to Ethernet and beyond, makes 
it adaptable to a wide range of platforms and payloads.
Qualification Testing
SKAIDOCK has been qualified for operation in a space 
environment. During thermal-vacuum (TVAC) testing, it was 
tested for operational temperatures between -40°C and +60°C. 
The board has also undergone sine and random vibration 
testing, shock testing, and radiated emissions measurements. 
The Xiphos Q8J module, with extensive flight heritage, has also 
been fully qualified.
