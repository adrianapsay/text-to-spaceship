COMMAND AND DATA HANDLING SYSTEM 
The BST CDH system consist of two main parts: the OBC-110 and the ACC-110. The OBC-
110 is responsible for scheduling all tasks and coordinating TMTC functions of the bus whereas 
the ACC-110 which is integrated in the same housing runs the attitude algorithm and connects 
to all ADCS components. Optionally an auxiliary processor can be attached to the OBC-110 
that allows centralized operation via a Linux operation system.
