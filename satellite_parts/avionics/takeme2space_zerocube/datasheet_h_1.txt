Zerocube - Technical specifications
Description:
ZeroCube is a CubeSat Onboard Computer (OBC) powered by Raspberry Pi and integrated
with NASA's cFS framework. It provides efficient and versatile computing for CubeSats,
allowing easy extension of both software and hardware capabilities. With its modular
software
design,
users
can
add
mission-specific
apps
without
compromising
OBC
performance, and it supports multiple I2C sensors. The built-in hardware watchdog ensures
reliability during long-duration missions.
